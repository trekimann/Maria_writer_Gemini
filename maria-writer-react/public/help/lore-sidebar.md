# Lore Sidebar

The **Lore** section in the sidebar lets you navigate and manage your world-building documents independently from your manuscript chapters.

## Usage

- **Drag and Drop**: Reorder lore entries by dragging them within the Lore section.
- **Add**: Click `+` next to the Lore heading, or click **New Lore Entry** at the bottom of the list.
- **Open**: Click a lore entry to open it in the editor.
- **Metadata**: Click the three-dot `⋮` icon next to an entry to set its title, date, related events, and mentioned characters.
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon to delete a lore entry (unlike chapters, all lore entries can be deleted).
