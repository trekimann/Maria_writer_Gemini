# Reader

The **Reader** opens your manuscript in a cleaner, page-based reading view so you can focus on the story instead of editing controls.

## Reader layout

The page is split into two main areas:

- **Library sidebar** — your projects, shared projects, chapters, and reader actions.
- **Reading pane** — the current chapter shown as real pages.

You can collapse the sidebar at any time from the top bar to give the manuscript more space.

## Sidebar sections

The sidebar includes collapsible sections:

- **Reader actions** — quick access to **Reviews** and **Invitations**.
- **Your stories** — projects saved to your account.
- **Shared with you** — projects another creator shared with you.
- **Chapters** — jump directly to a chapter in the open project.

## Reading a project

1. Select a project from **Your stories** or **Shared with you**.
2. Pick a chapter from the **Chapters** section.
3. Read using the page controls at the bottom of the manuscript.

The reader adapts to the available space:

- wider screens can show a two-page spread
- narrower screens fall back to a single-page layout

When you reach the first or last page of a chapter, the page buttons become chapter navigation.

## Font size

Use **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** in the top bar to change the reading size.

The reader repaginates automatically when the font size or window size changes.

## Reviews and suggestions

Open **Reviews** from the sidebar to show the review drawer.

If you have comment permission, select text in the manuscript to create a review note:

- leave a standard comment for feedback
- enable a suggestion to propose replacement wording

If you own the project, you can accept a suggestion directly from the review drawer and update the chapter text.

## Invitations

Use **Invitations** from the sidebar to manage project shares that were sent to you.

After you accept an invitation, the shared project appears automatically in the reader library.

## How this differs from the editor

The reader is optimized for reviewing and reading:

- editing tools are hidden
- formatting markup is stripped from the reading view
- the layout focuses on chapters, paging, and collaboration feedback
