# Invitations

The **Invitations** page collects project invites sent to your account email.

## Accept an invite

1. Open the invitation card.
2. Review the project title, owner, and access level.
3. Click **Accept**.

Accepted projects appear in your **Reader Library**.

## Decline an invite

Click **Decline** if you do not want access. The invitation is removed from your pending list.

## Invite links

Invitation links sent by a project owner open this page directly. If the link contains a token, Maria Writer highlights the matching invitation so you can act on it quickly.

## Notes

- Invitations are tied to the **email address** on your account.
- If an invite was sent to a different email, it cannot be accepted by your current account.
- Invitations expire automatically after a limited time.
