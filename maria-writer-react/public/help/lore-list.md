# Lore List

The **Lore** tab in the Codex gives you an overview of all your world-building documents.

## What is Lore?

Lore entries are detailed documents for your world's background — history, locations, factions, magic systems, cultures, and anything else that enriches your story. Unlike chapters, lore entries are not part of the manuscript narrative; they are reference material.

## Usage

- **Search**: Filter lore entries by title or content using the search bar.
- **Open**: Click any entry to open it in the editor.
- **Add**: Click **Add Lore Entry** (or the `+` button in the sidebar Lore section) to create a new entry.

## Tips

- Use the lore editor's **@mention** feature to tag characters, making it easy to link lore to the people in your story.
- Assign events via the Lore Entry Metadata panel to tie background lore to your timeline.
- Organise entries by naming them consistently (e.g. "Location: Ironhaven", "Faction: The Order").
